Shopify Australia vs Etsy Australia: What an A$48 Order Really Costs

For a seller in Australia, an A$48 order costs very different amounts depending on where it is sold. On Etsy, an Australian seller pays a $0.20 listing fee, a 6.5% transaction fee on the price plus shipping charged, and payment processing of 3% + A$0.25. On an A$48 order that is A$3.12 in transaction fees and A$1.69 in processing, plus the $0.20 listing fee — about A$4.81 in total, or just over 10% of the sale before postage, packaging, or the product itself.

Shopify Australia charges a different way. The Basic plan is A$56 a month, and the online standard card rate through Shopify Payments is 1.7% + 30¢ AUD. On an A$48 order the card fee is only about A$1.12 — the lowest card rate of any Shopify market in this comparison. The monthly plan is the deciding factor: spread across 20 orders a month it adds A$2.80 per order, for an all-in cost near A$3.92 — cheaper than Etsy. Spread across 50 orders it adds about A$1.12, and the all-in cost drops to about A$2.24, roughly half of Etsy's take. The breakeven sits at around 16 orders a month at this order value.

Below that breakeven, Etsy is the cheaper home because there is no monthly bill — you only pay when you sell. Above it, your own store wins, and the gap grows with every extra sale, because the plan cost per order keeps falling while Etsy's percentage fees never do. One cost to watch on Etsy is Offsite Ads: if the toggle is on, an ad-attributed sale costs an extra 15% of the price plus shipping — A$7.20 on an A$48 order — which can erase the margin on a sale that looked profitable.

The habit that saves money is comparing the full fee stack, not the headline rate. A marketplace advertising a low percentage can still cost more once listing, transaction and processing fees are added.
